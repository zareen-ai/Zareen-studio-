# 03 — Content Pillars + 30 Episode Ideas

## 1. Saat pillars (har video in me se ek ke andar)

| # | Pillar | Emotion | Kyun chalta hai | Share trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Rishton Ki Keemat** (maa, baap, bhai, saas-bahu) | Pyaar + dard | Sabse universal desi topic | "ye apni ammi ko bhejo" |
| P2 | **Paisa Aur Niyat** (rezaaq, imaandari, karz, lalach) | Insaaf | Gareeb/middle-class direct relate | "aisa aadmi aaj bhi chahiye" |
| P3 | **Chhoti Si Neki** (ek chhota acha kaam, bada nateeja) | Umeed | Feel-good, sabse zyada forward hota hai | WhatsApp status material |
| P4 | **Aurat Ki Taqat** (beti, bhabi, naukri, ghar) | Ghairat + himmat | Female audience ka core | "har beti ko ye sunao" |
| P5 | **Bachpan Ki Seekh** (baap ki nasihat, school, galti) | Nostalgia | 30+ audience, strong recall | "mere abbu bhi aise kehte the" |
| P6 | **Karma / Waqt Ka Pahiya** | Chot + sabr | Story twist ka best vehicle | "waqt sabko sabak sikhata hai" |
| P7 | **Shukr Aur Sabar** (jo hai us pe khush) | Sukoon | Raat ke time sabse zyada chalta hai | "dil ko sukoon dene wali baat" |

**Mix rule:** hafte me 7 video = P1, P6, P3, P1, P4, P2, P7.
Matlab har hafte 2 baar P1 (sabse strong pillar) aur ek baar P7 (weekend sukoon).

## 3. Idea generation ka formula (jab list khatam ho)

Nayi kahani banane ke liye sirf ye 3 sawal pucho:

1. **Ghar ka kaunsa ek chhota moment hai jisme chhupa hua dard hai?**
   (roti, chabi, tiffin, joote, phone call, chhat, dukaan, bus stop)
2. **Us moment ka "asli matlab" kya hai jo tab samajh nahi aaya tha?**
3. **Ek line ka moral kya hai jo sun-ne wala khud apne ghar pe apply kar sake?**

In teeno ka jawab aa gaya = episode ready.

**Bonus sources:**
- YouTube/Instagram comments — audience apni kahani khud likhti hai
- WhatsApp groups ke forwarded messages
- Purane Urdu digest (Khawateen Digest, Shuaa) — public domain ideas
- Nani/dadi se suni hui baatein

## 4. Series formats (Phase 2 me)

| Series | Format | Kyun |
|---|---|---|

| **Mohalle Ki Khidki** | Har episode ek ghar ki kahani | Alag alag characters, same narrator |
| **Aap Ne Bheja** | Audience ki bheji hui kahani | UGC engine — comments se content |
| **3 Din, 3 Sabak** | Ek kahani 3 parts me | Retention + follow-for-part-2 |
