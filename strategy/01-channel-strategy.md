# 01 — Channel Strategy

## 1. Ek line me channel

> **Noor** — ek 24 saal ki desi ladki, roz camera pe 45 second me ek asli zindagi ki kahani
> sunati hai, aur end me ek aisi baat keh jati hai jo seedha dil pe lagti hai.

Naam ka option: channel ka naam **Zareen Studio** rakho aur series ka naam **"Noor Ki Kahani"**.
Isse brand bada rehta hai (baad me doosre characters aa sakte hain) aur series pehchani rehti hai.

---

## 2. Audience kaun hai

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Pakistan + India, 25–55 saal, mobile-first, WhatsApp-forwarding audience |
| **Core feeling** | "Arey ye to mere ghar ki baat hai" |
| **Language** | Roman Urdu + Hindi mix (Hinglish). Na shuddh Hindi, na adabi Urdu |
| **Watch time** | Raat 8–11 baje, aur subah 7–9 baje (chai time) |
| **Behaviour** | Comment me apni kahani likhte hain, video WhatsApp status pe lagate hain |

**Sabse bada insight:** is audience ke liye *share* karna hi *like* karna hai.
Har video me ek aisi line honi chahiye jo forward karne layak ho —
"ye bhejo us maa ko jo..." type. Isi se algorithm chalta hai.

---

## 3. Niche ka gap (hum kyun jeetenge)

Market me already hai:
- ❌ Robotic AI voice + stock footage wali kahaniyan (boring, koi trust nahi)
- ❌ Bahut cinematic, bahut adabi Urdu (aam viewer se connect nahi hota)
- ❌ Sirf text-on-screen moral quotes (retention gir jata hai)

**Humara gap:** ek *consistent, pehchana hua chehra* jo selfie-style camera pe baat kare —
matlab UGC ki asliyat + AI ki scale. Desi short-story space me consistent AI UGC host
abhi bhi khaali jagah hai. Yahi moat hai: **chehra roz wahi, awaaz roz wahi.**

---

## 4. Format spec (fix kar liya hai, badalna nahi)

| Item | Value | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **9:16 vertical** | Shorts/Reels/TikTok teeno |
| Length | **40–60 second** (sweet spot 45s) | Poori kahani + moral, retention safe |
| Style | Talking head, selfie/handheld energy | UGC trust |
| Frame | Chest-up, aankhein lens me | Direct connection |
| Background | Wahi kahani-wala corner (har video me) | Instant recognition |
| Captions | Bade Roman Urdu/Hinglish words, keyword highlight | 70% log sound off dekhte hain |
| Posting | Roz 1 video, raat 8:30 PM PKT / 9:00 PM IST | Consistency > volume |

**Har video ka structure (ye hi formula, 100 videos tak):**

```
0–3s   HOOK        — sawal ya adhoori baat. Chehra lens me, haath hilta hai.
3–10s  SETUP       — kahani ka scene: "jab main chhoti thi..."
10–25s CONFLICT    — wo moment jab galat ya dukh hua
25–38s TWIST       — asli baat saamne aati hai (ye sabse zaroori beat hai)
38–45s MORAL       — ek line, dheemi awaaz, pause ke saath
45–50s CTA         — "comment me batao" / "ye kisi maa ko bhejo"
```

---

## 5. Retention ke 6 rules

1. **Pehla frame me chehra aur haath dono dikhne chahiye.** Blank frame = swipe.
2. **Hook me kahani ka end mat batao.** Sirf chot dikhao: "Maa ne kaha, maine kha liya."
3. **Har 4 second me kuch badle** — cut, zoom, expression, ya naya B-roll clip.
4. **Awaz me ek pause zaroor rakho** twist se pehle. Silence retention badhata hai.
5. **Moral ko lecture mat banao.** Ek line, kahani ke andar se nikli hui.
6. **CTA me sawal pucho, "follow karo" mat kaho.** Comment = reach.

---

## 6. Cross-posting (ek video, teen jagah)

| Platform | Kya badalna hai |
|---|---|
| **YouTube Shorts** | Title me keyword: "Maa ne kyu kaha maine kha liya 😢 #shorts". Pinned comment me poori moral. |
| **Instagram Reels** | Caption me 3-line kahani + sawal. Hashtag 8–12. Cover pe text hook. |
| **TikTok** | Caption chhota, hashtag 4–6. Sound trending hona chahiye ya original voice. |

**Watermark rule:** TikTok ka watermark laga video YouTube pe mat daalo — reach kat'ti hai.
Hamesha clean master file se export karo, phir teeno pe alag caption ke saath upload.

Ek hi video teeno pe, lekin **upload time 30 minute ka gap** rakho
(8:30 YT → 9:00 IG → 9:30 TT).

---

## 7. Growth engine (0 → 35K tak)

**Phase 1 (Din 1–30) — Pehchaan banao**
- Roz 1 video, bina gap. 20 video minimum.
- Sab kuch same: corner, kapde, awaaz, caption style.
- Goal: ek video ka 50K+ jaana. Uska format 3 baar dohrao.

**Phase 2 (Din 31–60) — Jo chala usko double karo**
- Top 3 videos ka topic 2-2 aur variants banao.
- Series shuru karo: "Saas-Bahu ki sachhi kahaniyan — Part 1, 2, 3..."
- Reply-to-comment videos banao (ye sabse sasta viral format hai).

**Phase 3 (Din 61–90) — Scale aur paisa**
- Doosra character introduce karo (buzurg narrator) — same universe.
- Long-form: 3–5 min wali kahani YouTube pe (Shorts se traffic).
- Monetization: YouTube Partner, brand deals (chai, kapde, apps),
  aur apni compiled kahani ki PDF/ebook.

---

## 8. KPI dashboard (har hafte Sunday ko dekho)

| Metric | Target | Kaise theek karein agar kam ho |
|---|---|---|
| Avg view duration | > 70% | Hook badlo, pehle 3 second pe kaam karo |
| 3-second retention | > 75% | Pehla frame + pehli line badlo |
| Shares per 1000 views | > 15 | Story me "forward-worthy" line daalo |
| Comments per 1000 views | > 8 | End me sawal pucho |
| Follower conversion | > 1.5% | Corner/chehra consistent rakho |

---

## 9. Jo bilkul nahi karna (brand safety)

- ❌ Kisi mazhab, caste, ya mulk ke khilaaf baat — desi audience me ye sabse tez jhagda hai
- ❌ Politics, election, fauj
- ❌ Asli logon ki burai (naam lekar)
- ❌ Khoon, accident, maut ki graphic detail — platform reach maar deta hai
- ❌ Clickbait jo story se match na kare
- ✅ Har kahani ka end umeed pe ho. Dukh dikhao, lekin raasta bhi dikhao.
