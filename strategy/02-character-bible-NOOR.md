## 2. Look (ye bilkul fix hai — prompt me hamesha yahi lines)

```
South Asian young woman, 24 years old, warm wheat-brown skin with natural texture
and visible pores, oval face, soft full cheeks, small nose, dark brown almond eyes
with subtle kohl liner, thick natural eyebrows, faint dark circles,
long black hair in a loose low braid with a few strands falling on her face,
small gold jhumka earrings, tiny gold nose stud on the left nostril,
mustard-yellow cotton kurta, thin maroon dupatta loosely over one shoulder,
light henna on her hands, no makeup look, simple and real
```

**Kapde ka rule:**
- Default: **mustard-yellow kurta + maroon dupatta** (ye uska signature hai)
- Winter episodes: upar se ek grey shawl
- Special episode (Eid/wedding): white kurta + green dupatta
- **Kabhi nahi:** western outfit, heavy makeup, jewellery ka dher, branded logo

**Kyun mustard + maroon?** 9:16 chhote screen pe ye do rang sabse pehle pakde jaate hain,
aur desi ghar ke andar warm light me ye natural lagte hain.

## 3. Setting (har video ka background — yahi rahega)

```
a cozy corner of a modest South Asian home, warm cream textured wall,
soft warm fairy lights blurred in the background, a wooden shelf with old books,
a small brass oil lamp, a steel cup of chai on a low table, a window with thin
sunlight or warm evening tungsten light, slightly cluttered and lived-in,
shallow depth of field, background softly out of focus
```

**Badalna allowed hai:**
- Subah wali kahani → window se thin sunlight
- Raat wali kahani → tungsten lamp + fairy lights
- Baarish wali kahani → window pe rain streaks

**Nahi badalna:** wall ka rang, shelf, chai ka cup, fairy lights.

## 4. Camera language (UGC feel)

| Shot | Kab | Flow prompt phrase |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Selfie MCU** | Hook + Moral | `vertical 9:16 medium close-up, chest-up, handheld selfie energy, subtle natural shake, looking straight into the lens` |
| **B. Locked tripod MCU** | Kahani sunate waqt | `vertical 9:16 medium close-up on a locked tripod, chest-up, slight slow push-in` |
| **C. Tight CU** | Emotional twist | `vertical 9:16 tight close-up on her face and eyes, shallow depth of field, slow push-in` |
| **D. Hand/prop insert** | Beat change | `vertical 9:16 close-up of her hands holding a steel cup of chai, steam rising` |

**Rule:** ek clip me sirf **ek** camera move. "Push-in then pan then zoom" mat likhna —
Flow confuse ho jata hai.

## 5. Voice & bolne ka style

- **Language:** Roman Urdu + Hindi mix. Roz-marra ke words.
- **Speed:** normal se thoda dheema. Kahani sunane wali raftaar, news anchor wali nahi.
- **Tone:** apni behen jaisi — pyaar, thoda gussa, thoda mazaaq.
- **Signature habits:**
  - Kahani shuru: **"Ek baat suno..."** ya **"Aaj jo kahani sunane wali hoon..."**
  - Pause marker: **"..."** ya **"ruk jao"**
  - Moral ka pehla word hamesha dheema: **"Aur sach ye hai ke..."**
  - CTA: **"Comment me batao — tumhare ghar me aisa hua hai?"**
- **Forbidden words:** "dosto", "guys", "subscribe karo jaldi", "viral", "shocking",
  "namaskar", "assalam o alaikum" har video me (kabhi kabhi theek hai, formula mat banao)

**Voice consistency ke liye:** ek hi TTS voice ya ek hi insaan ki awaaz — 100 videos tak.
Details: `production/02-audio-voice-strategy.md`

## 8. Character ki back-story (script likhte waqt yaad rakho)

Noor ki ammi roz raat ko sabke baad khana khati hain — aur aksar kehti hain "maine kha liya".
Noor ki nani ke paas ek purani diary hai jis me mohalle ki kahaniyan likhi hain.
Noor ne pehli kahani usi diary se padhi thi. Isi liye uska series ka naam hai
**"Ammi Ki Diary"** — aur isi se uske paas kabhi kahaniyan khatam nahi hoti.

Ye back-story kabhi kabhi video me bhi aayegi (episode 20 ke aas-paas) —
audience ko lagega ke unka character bhi badh raha hai. **Yehi parasocial growth hai.**
