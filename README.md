# Zareen Studio — Desi Kahaniyan (AI UGC Channel)

Ek AI UGC character (**Noor**) jo Roman Urdu/Hinglish me chhoti chhoti moral stories sunati hai —
YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels aur TikTok teeno pe ek saath.

Video generation: **Google Flow (Veo 3.1)**.
Ye repo channel ka poora dimaag hai — strategy, scripts, aur copy-paste-ready Flow prompts.

---

## 🚀 Aaj hi shuru karna hai? Sirf 4 step

| Step | Kya karna hai | File |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Channel + character decide karo (already done — padh lo) | `strategy/01-channel-strategy.md` |
| **2** | Character ki 3 reference images banao (ye sabse zaroori step hai) | `prompts/01-character-ingredient-prompts.md` |
| **3** | Episode 1 ka script padho, voice record/TTS karo | `scripts/EP01-maa-kehti-hai-kha-liya.md` |
| **4** | 6 clips ke prompts Flow me paste karo, edit me jodo | `prompts/03-EP01-flow-prompt-pack.md` |

Uske baad har naye episode ke liye: `production/01-episode-workflow.md` follow karo.

## ⚡ Do rules jo kabhi mat todna

1. **Har video me ek hi chehra.** Flow me hamesha Noor ki ingredient images attach karo —
   text-only prompt se chehra badal jata hai aur audience ka trust toot'ta hai.
2. **Har video me ek hi moral, ek hi emotion.** Do baatein ek saath mat khao.
   45 second me ek chot lagti hai — wahi chot lagao.
