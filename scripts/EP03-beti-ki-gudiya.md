# EP03 — Beti ki gudiya

## Meta

| | |
|---|---|
| **EP #** | 03 |
| **Pillar** | P3 — Chhoti Si Neki |
| **Emotion** | Sharminda + pyaar + seekh |
| **Length** | ~50 second |
| **Clips** | 6 × 8s |
| **Ingredients** | slot 1 `noor-front` · slot 2 `corner-background` |
| **Dialogue mode** | Option B (silent + VO) |

**Logline:** Noor ne ek roti hui 5 saal ki bachi ko tasalli dene ke liye jhoot bol diya —
"gudiya theek ho jayegi." Bachi ne jawab me wo cheez de di jo uske paas sabse qeemti thi.

**Moral:** *Bachche jhoot maaf kar dete hain. Bewaqoofi nahi.*

## Voiceover (Roman)

```
Maine paanch saal ki bachi se jhoot bola... aur us ne mujhe sabak sikha diya.

Gali me ek bachi thi. Us ki gudiya ka haath toot gaya tha.
Wo ro rahi thi, jaise us ka sab kuch toot gaya ho.

Mujhse raha nahi gaya. Maine kaha —
"Gudiya theek ho jayegi, main kal lakar dungi."
Wo chup ho gayi.

Phir us ne apni jeb se ek toffee nikali... aur mere haath pe rakh di.
Boli — "Aap ke liye. Aap meri gudiya theek karogi na."

Main wo gudiya kabhi theek nahi kar payi.
Lekin us din samajh aaya —
bachche jhoot maaf kar dete hain. Bewaqoofi nahi.

Aaj bhi wo toffee mere drawer me hai.
Comment me batao — kisi bachche ne tumhe kabhi sharminda kiya hai?
```
