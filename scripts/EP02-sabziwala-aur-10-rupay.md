**Logline:** Ek sabziwale ne Noor se 10 rupay kam liye aur wazan zyada diya. Jab Noor ne

## Clip-specific prompt notes

Zyada tar clips wahi shot library se aayenge
(`prompts/02-shot-library-flow-formula.md`). Sirf ye lines badalni hain:

**Clip 1 — prop add karo:**
```
She holds up a crumpled ten-rupee banknote between her thumb and finger, close to
her chest, and looks at it for a moment before looking into the lens.
```
+ Ingredient slot 3 me ek clean 10 rupay note ki image daal sakti ho to daalo.
Na ho to `a crumpled small denomination banknote, no readable text or numbers` likho.

**Clip 2 — B-roll (E1):**
```

Lighting: warm golden-hour street light, long soft shadows, slightly hazy.
Camera: locked tripod, no zoom, no pan.
Audio: street ambience — bicycle bells, a distant vendor's call, a passing motorbike,
no dialogue, no music.
Style: realistic documentary UGC footage, natural texture, warm colour grade,

No text, no captions, no signage text, no logos, no watermark.

```

```
One continuous shot.
```

## Voiceover (Roman)

```
Us sabziwale ne mujhse das rupay kam liye... aur main paanch saal se soch rahi hoon.

Mohalle ke corner pe ek thela hai. Ek buzurg aadmi, safed kurta, kamzor haath.
Sabzi tolta hai.

Maine do kilo tamatar kaha. Usne dhai kilo tol diye. Aur bill banaya — kam.

Maine kaha — "Chacha, aap ne galti kar di."
Wo hanse. Bole — "Galti nahi beti."

Phir jo unhone kaha...
"Pehli baar kisi ne mujhse bina mol-bhaav kiya hai.
Baaki sab das rupay ke liye meri izzat utaar dete hain."

Aur sach ye hai ke — imaandari abhi bhi zinda hai.
Bas use pehchanne wala chahiye.

Comment me batao — tumhe aisa koi anjaan insaan mila hai?
```
