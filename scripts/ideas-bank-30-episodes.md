## EP04 — Baap ke joote (P1)

**Hook:** "Mere abbu ke joote 6 saal purane the."

**Beats:**
1. Abbu ke joote — sole pe tape, colour uda hua. Noor bachpan me sharminda hoti thi.
2. Har saal school ke liye Noor ke naye joote aate the. Abbu kehte "abhi chalte hain".
3. Ek din Noor ne zid ki — "Aap naye joote kyun nahi lete?" Abbu muskuraye, baat taal di.
4. **Twist:** Abbu ke guzar jaane ke baad almari saaf karte waqt ek dibba mila —
   andar **Noor ke naam ke naye joote**, uske agle saal ke size ke. Wo khud ke liye
   paise bacha hi nahi rahe the.
5. **Moral:** Baap ki zarooratein dafan ho jati hain — taake bachchon ki poori hon.

**Shots:** A1 (hook) · E1 (purane joote, darwaze pe, chehra nahi) · D1 (tape lagate haath) ·
B1 (conflict) · D2/E1 (almari ka dibba, naye joote) · C1 (twist) · G1 (moral) · A1 (CTA)

**Props:** purane leather joote + cello tape · naye joote ka dibba

## EP05 — Cycle ka scratch (P5)

**Hook:** "Maine cycle girayi — aur jhoot bol diya."

**Beats:**
1. 10 saal ki Noor ne abbu ki cycle chhupa ke chalayi, deewar se takra gayi, scratch lag gaya.
2. Darr ke maare boli — "kisi aur ne girayi."
3. Abbu ne kuch nahi kaha. Chup chap scratch pe polish lagayi.
4. **Twist:** Raat ko abbu bole — "Cycle to main theek kar deta, beta.
   Mujhe dukh is baat ka hai ke tum ne mujhse darr ke jhoot bola.
   Mujhse darna mat — main tumhara baap hoon, judge nahi."
5. **Moral:** Galti maaf ho jati hai. Jhoot nahi. Aur bachcha tab jhoot bolta hai
   jab use lage ke sach bolne pe pyaar kam ho jayega.

**Shots:** A1 · E1 (cycle deewar ke saath, bachcha peeche se) · D1 (polish lagate haath) ·
B1 · C1 (twist — abbu ki line) · G1 · A1

## EP06 — Beti padhegi (P4)

**Hook:** "Chacha ne kaha tha — beti ko itna padhana kya?"

**Beats:**
1. Gaon me chacha ne Noor ki saheli ke baap ko taana diya: "Beti ko itna padhana kya,
   aakhir to paraye ghar jayegi."
2. Saheli ne chhat pe mombatti jala ke padha. Log haste rahe.
3. 8 saal beet gaye.
4. **Twist:** Wahi chacha ek sarkari daftar me file le kar khade hain —
   aur kursi ke peeche naam likha hai: **District Collector — us saheli ka naam.**
   Chacha ne sar jhuka ke kaha "beti". Wo boli — "Chacha, baithiye. Aap ka kaam ho jayega."
5. **Moral:** Waqt har sawal ka jawab deta hai. Bas tab tak padhte raho.

**Shots:** A1 · E1 (chhat, mombatti, larki ki peeth) · B1 · E1 (sarkari daftar,
file, kursi ka nameplate — text readable mat karo) · C1 · G1 · A1

## EP07 — Rickshaw wale ka beta (P2)

**Hook:** "Ek rickshaw wale ne meri fees bhari."

**Beats:**
1. Noor ke bhai ki school fees 3 mahine se baki. Ghar me paise nahi.
2. School se nikalte waqt bhai ro raha tha. Ek anjaan rickshaw wala dekh raha tha.
3. Agli subah fees jama ho chuki thi — receipt pe kisi anjaan ka naam.
4. 10 saal baad, Noor ki ammi bimaar. Hospital me ek doctor ilaaj karta hai,
   bill maaf kar deta hai.
5. **Twist:** Jab Noor ne poocha "kyun?", doctor bola —
   "Mere abbu rickshaw chalate the. Ek baar unhone kisi bachche ki fees bhari thi.
   Unhone kaha tha — jo mila hai, lautana padta hai."
6. **Moral:** Neki kabhi bekar nahi jati. Wo ghum ke wapas aati hai —
   aksar us waqt jab tumhe sabse zyada zaroorat hoti hai.

**Shots:** A1 · E1 (rickshaw, peeth) · D1 (school receipt, text unreadable) ·
B1 · E1 (hospital corridor) · C1 · G1 · A1

## EP08 — Bhai ka aadha sandwich (P1)

**Hook:** "Mera bhai hamesha kehta tha — mujhe bhook nahi."

**Beats:**
1. School ka tiffin — ek sandwich, do bachche. Bhai hamesha aadha de deta, kehta "mujhe bhook nahi".
2. Noor khush hoti — use zyada milta tha.
3. Badi ho ke Noor sheher chali gayi, bhai gaon me raha. Calls kam ho gaye.
4. **Twist:** Ek din bhai ki shaadi pe, uski saali ne kaha — "Inhe bachpan se
   sandwich pasand hi nahi tha, phir bhi roz ek extra mangwate the."
   Noor ko tab samajh aaya — bhai kabhi bhookha nahi tha. Wo bas apni behen ko khilata tha.
5. **Moral:** Chhoti si mohabbat kabhi chhoti nahi hoti. Aur jo chup ke de,
   use kabhi shukriya nahi milta — isliye khud kehna padta hai.

**Shots:** A1 · D1 (do haath, aadha sandwich) · E1 (school gate, bachche) ·
B1 · C1 · G1 · A1

## EP09 — Ek cup chai (P3)

**Hook:** "Wo chai maine uske liye nahi banayi thi."

**Beats:**
1. Baarish. Noor dukan pe chhat ke neeche khadi thi. Ek mazdoor bheeg raha tha, thithur raha tha.
2. Noor ne chai wale se kaha — "ek chai isko de do, paise main deti hoon."
   Us ne soch bhi nahi tha.
3. Mazdoor ne chai li, ek ghoont piya, phir ruk gaya. Aankhein bhar aayin.
4. **Twist:** Us ne kaha — "Behen, teen saal me pehli baar kisi ne mujhse poocha
   ke mujhe chai chahiye ya nahi. Sab sirf kaam poochte hain."
5. **Moral:** Ek cup chai 10 rupay ki hoti hai. Lekin kisi ka poora din bacha leti hai.

**Shots:** A1 · F1 (baarish, window) · E1 (chhat ke neeche mazdoor, chehra shadow me) ·
D1 (chai ka glass, bhaap) · C1 · G1 · A1

## EP10 — Padosan ki chabi (P6)

**Hook:** "Padosan ne meri chabi chhupa ke hasi thi."

**Beats:**
1. Noor ki ammi bimaar, Noor 14 saal ki. Ghar ki chabi kho gayi.
2. Padosan ko pata tha chabi kahan hai — us ne bataya nahi, has ke kaha "dhundhti raho".
3. Poori raat barish me dhundha. Subah chabi mili — wahan jahan padosan ne rakhi thi.
4. Do saal beet gaye.
5. **Twist:** Wahi padosan Noor ke darwaze pe aati hai — uske shohar ka ilaaj nahi ho
   raha, paise chahiye. Noor ne kuch nahi kaha, andar bulaya, chai di, aur paise de diye.
   Padosan ro padi. Noor boli — "Aap ne mujhe chabi dhoondhna sikha diya tha.
   Ab main aap ka raasta dhoondh dungi."
6. **Moral:** Waqt sab ko ek hi jagah le aata hai. Fark sirf itna hai —
   koi wahan badla lene aata hai, koi madad karne.

**Shots:** A1 · D1 (chabi, barish me haath) · E1 (gali, padosan ka darwaza, chehra nahi) ·
B1 · C1 · G1 · A1

## Idea kahan se layein (jab bank khatam ho)

1. **Comments padho** — audience apni kahani khud likhti hai. Sabse tez source.
2. **WhatsApp forwards** — desi groups me roz kahaniyan ghoomti hain
3. **Purane Urdu digest** — Khawateen Digest, Shuaa, Kahani (idea lo, copy mat karo)
4. **Sabzi mandi / bus stand / hospital waiting room** — asli kahaniyan yahan milti hain
5. **Apni nani/dadi se poocho** — "aap ke mohalle me kaun si sabse ajeeb baat hui thi?"
6. **News ke chhote tukde** — crime nahi, insaniyat wale hisse

**Har naye idea ko 3 sawal se test karo:**
- Kya ye kisi ek ghar ki baat hai jo sabke ghar se milti hai?
- Kya isme ek twist hai jo pehle 20 second me guess na ho?
- Kya iska moral ek line me likha ja sakta hai?

Teeno "haan" → episode ready.
