# 04 — Hook + Caption Library

> Ye channel ka **arsenal** hai. Script likhte waqt block ho jao to yahan se uthao.
> Har hook pehle 3 second me bola jata hai — isi pe 70% retention tiki hai.

---

## 1. 8 hook formulas (+ 30 ready lines)

### Formula A — Adhoori baat (sabse strong)
Batao kya hua, kyun nahi.

1. "Maa ne kabhi nahi kaha ke use bhook lagi hai."
2. "Us sabziwale ne mujhse 10 rupay kam liye."
3. "Mere abbu ke joote 6 saal purane the."
4. "Maine paanch saal ki bachi se jhoot bola."
5. "Wo chai maine uske liye nahi banayi thi."
6. "Baap ne marne se pehle ek cheque chhupa ke rakha tha."

### Formula B — Confession (sharminda)
Noor apni galti maanti hai — audience turant judti hai.

7. "Maine apni maa ko 3 saal se phone nahi kiya."
8. "Maine us din jhoot bola... aur aaj tak nahi bataya."
9. "Main us aurat ko galat samajhti thi."
10. "Mujhe ek baat pe pachtawa hai — roz raat ko."
11. "Maine apne bhai ko kabhi shukriya nahi kaha."

### Formula C — Ulti baat (contrarian)
Jo sab maante hain, use tod do.

12. "Gareeb log zyada generous hote hain — maine khud dekha."
13. "Jhoot bolna kabhi kabhi sabse bada pyaar hota hai."
14. "Sab ne kaha wo toot jayegi. Wo tooti nahi — bani."
15. "Bara ghar hona khushi nahi hoti."
16. "Achi bahu banne ki koshish mat karo."

### Formula D — Seedha sawal
Viewer ko sochne pe majboor.

17. "Aap ki ammi ne aakhri baar apne liye kya khareeda?"
18. "Kab aap ne kisi gareeb se bina mol-bhaav kiya?"
19. "Aap ke abbu ki sabse purani cheez kaun si hai?"
20. "Kya aap ne kabhi kisi ko chhup ke khate dekha hai?"

### Formula E — Time jump
"Aaj" aur "us din" ka farq.

21. "5 saal pehle us ne mujhe ek toffee di thi. Aaj bhi rakhi hai."
22. "20 saal pehle maine wo tiffin kisi ko de diya tha. Kal wo laut aaya."
23. "10 saal lag gaye mujhe ye baat samajhne me."
24. "Aaj wahi aadmi mere saamne khada tha."

### Formula F — Ek cheez ka zikr (object hook)
Ek chhota object — poori kahani us me band.

25. "Meri nani ki sandook me sirf ek cheez thi."
26. "Ye chabi 15 saal se mere paas hai."
27. "Wo ek roti aaj bhi mujhe yaad hai."
28. "Abbu ki dukan ka wo khata kabhi band nahi hua."

### Formula G — Sun-ne ka hukm (Noor ka signature)
29. "Ek baat suno... ruk jao, ye zaroori hai."
30. "Aaj jo kahani sunane wali hoon — wo mere ghar ki hai."

## 2. Hook likhne ke 4 rules

| Rule | Kyun |
|---|---|
| **Pehle 6 words me subject clear ho** | Scroll rukta hai pehle 1 second me |
| **Kahani ka end mat batao** | Jawab mil gaya to video band |
| **Number ya concrete object daalo** | "10 rupay", "6 saal", "ek roti" — dimaag pakadta hai |
| **Emotion word avoid karo** | "Dukhi kahani" mat kaho — dukh dikhao |

**Bad hook:** "Aaj main aapko ek bahut emotional kahani sunane wali hoon."
**Good hook:** "Mere abbu ke joote 6 saal purane the."

## 3. CTA lines (end ke liye — rotate karo)

### Sawal wale (best for comments)
- "Comment me batao — tumhare ghar me aisa hua hai?"
- "Aap ko ye baat kab samajh aayi?"
- "Aap ke mohalle me aisa koi hai?"
- "Kya aap ne kabhi aisa kiya hai?"
- "Aap ki ammi ki kaun si aadat aapko yaad aa gayi?"

### Forward wale (best for shares)
- "Ye video apni ammi ko bhejo. Aaj hi."
- "Kisi aisi beti ko bhejo jo door rehti hai."
- "Apne bhai ko tag karo — kuch nahi likhna, bas bhej dena."
- "Ye kahani kisi us baap ko sunao jo kabhi kuch maangta nahi."

### Series wale (best for follows)
- "Diary ka agla panna — kal raat 8:30."
- "Ye kahani adhoori hai. Part 2 chahiye to comment me likho."
- "Mohalle ki agla ghar — kal."
