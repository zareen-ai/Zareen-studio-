# 03 — QC Checklist (upload se pehle, har video)

> Isse skip mat karna. Ek kharab video 20 achhi videos ka nuksan karti hai.

---

## A. Character consistency (sabse pehle ye)

- [ ] Chehra har clip me **same** lag raha hai (aankhein, naak, skin tone)
- [ ] Baal: loose low braid, har clip me
- [ ] Kapde: mustard kurta + maroon dupatta (ya episode ka decided outfit)
- [ ] Jhumka + nose stud present
- [ ] Background: wahi cream wall + fairy lights + shelf
- [ ] Skin texture natural — plastic/smooth nahi
- [ ] Haath sahi — 5 ungliyan, koi distortion nahi
- [ ] Koi extra character galti se frame me nahi aa gaya

**Agar chehra badla hua hai → us clip ko regenerate karo. Compromise mat karo.**

---

## B. Pehle 3 second (retention)

- [ ] Pehla frame me chehra **aur** haath dono dikh rahe hain
- [ ] Pehli line adhoori hai — jawab nahi deti
- [ ] Koi blank/black frame nahi
- [ ] Koi intro, logo animation, "namaskar" nahi
- [ ] Caption pehle second se hi shuru ho jata hai

---

## C. Script & story

- [ ] Sirf **ek** twist hai (do nahi)
- [ ] Twist se pehle 0.5–1s ka pause hai
- [ ] Moral **ek line** hai — lecture nahi
- [ ] Moral kahani ke andar se nikla hai, bahar se thopa hua nahi
- [ ] Ending umeed pe hai (brand safety rule)
- [ ] Length 40–60 second ke andar

---

## D. Audio

- [ ] Voice loudness ≈ **−14 LUFS**, peak −6 dB se neeche
- [ ] Music voice ke neeche (−18 dB), koi word dabaya hua nahi
- [ ] Twist pe music **mute**
- [ ] Koi word katra hua nahi
- [ ] Awaaz wahi hai jo pichhli video me thi
- [ ] Phone speaker pe sun ke test kiya

---

## E. Captions & text

- [ ] Roman Urdu/Hinglish spelling sahi
- [ ] Har 2–3 second me caption badalta hai
- [ ] Highlight words maroon/bold me
- [ ] Font size phone pe readable (chhota nahi)
- [ ] Caption safe area me hai — YouTube/IG ke UI ke neeche nahi chhupa
  (upar 15% aur neeche 20% khaali rakho)
- [ ] Koi AI-generated fake Urdu/Hindi text frame me nahi

---

## F. Brand safety

- [ ] Koi mazhab/caste/mulk ke khilaaf baat nahi
- [ ] Koi politics nahi
- [ ] Koi asli insaan ka naam le kar burai nahi
- [ ] Khoon/maut ka graphic detail nahi
- [ ] Kisi community ko target nahi karta
- [ ] Clickbait aur kahani match karte hain
- [ ] Bachchon ka koi unsafe depiction nahi

---

## G. Technical

- [ ] 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
- [ ] 30 fps, H.264, high bitrate
- [ ] File size < 200 MB
- [ ] **Koi TikTok watermark nahi** (cross-post karte waqt)
- [ ] Pehla frame clean hai (cover image ke liye)
- [ ] File name: `EP##-slug-1080x1920.mp4`

---

## H. Platform copy

- [ ] YT title ≤ 60 char, keyword + emoji + `#shorts`
- [ ] IG caption: hook + story + moral + sawal + 9–10 hashtags
- [ ] TikTok caption chhota + 4–6 hashtags
- [ ] IG cover text ready
- [ ] Pinned comment ready
- [ ] Comment reply templates yaad (`templates/caption-hashtag-template.md`)

---

## I. Final 3 sawal (upload se theek pehle)

1. **Kya main ye video khud apni ammi ko bhejungi?**
   Nahi → kuch kami hai.
2. **Kya pehle 3 second me main khud ruk jati?**
   Nahi → hook badlo.
3. **Kya moral ek line me yaad reh jayega?**
   Nahi → moral chhota karo.

---

## Agar video fail ho jaye

| Metric | Kya kharab hua | Kya badlo |
|---|---|---|
| 3s retention < 60% | Hook | Pehli line + pehla frame |
| Avg view < 50% | Pacing | Clips chhote karo, cuts tez |
| Views hain, share nahi | Story | "Forward-worthy" line add karo |
| Views hain, follower nahi | Identity | Corner/chehra/name consistency |
| Comment nahi | CTA | End me khula sawal pucho |

**Kabhi poori video blame mat karo — sirf ek cheez badlo aur dobara test karo.**
