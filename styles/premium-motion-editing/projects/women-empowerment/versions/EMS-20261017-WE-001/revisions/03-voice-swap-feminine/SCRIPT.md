## Roman Urdu reading

Women empowerment ka matlab special treatment nahi. Matlab har aurat ko apni zindagi ke faislay karne ka haq aur barabar mauqa milna hai. Taleem, career, income, apni awaaz — yeh uske choices hain, izzat aur safety ke saath. Jab larki seekh sakti hai, lead kar sakti hai, aur apne goals choose kar sakti hai, to uski salahiyat ko raasta milta hai. Yeh kisi ke khilaaf nahi, unfair barriers ke khilaaf hai. Equal opportunity se families aur communities mazboot hoti hain. Asal empowerment tab hai jab har larki ko apni raah chunne aur aage barhne ka haq mile.

## Editorial note

No numbers or unverifiable anecdotes are used. The message defines empowerment as equal choice and opportunity, not dominance. On-screen headlines are a visual emphasis, not word-for-word subtitles; no subtitle track is burned in.
