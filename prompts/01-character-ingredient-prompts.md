# 01 — Character Ingredient Prompts (SABSE PEHLE YE KARO)

> Ye 3 images **Noor ki pehchaan** hain. Har video ke har clip me inhe
> Flow ke **Ingredients to Video** me attach karogi. Iske bina chehra har clip
> me badal jayega aur audience ka trust toot jayega.

## Kaam ka tareeqa

1. Flow kholo → **Ingredients** → **New ingredient** → **Generate with Imagen**
2. Neeche diya prompt paste karo → generate
3. 4–6 variations banao, **sabse natural, front-facing, clean-background wala chuno**
4. Chuni hui image ko download karke `assets/character-sheets/` me save karo:
   - `noor-front.png`
   - `noor-three-quarter.png`
   - `noor-full-seated.png`
   - `corner-background.png`
5. In 4 ko Flow me **save kar lo** — ab kabhi dobara banane ki zaroorat nahi

> **Zaroori:** ye images ek hi session me, ek hi style me banao.
> Alag alag din banaoge to lighting/skin tone mismatch ho jayega.

---

## INGREDIENT 1 — `noor-front` (PRIMARY — hamesha pehli slot me)

Neutral front-facing, dialogue ke liye best. Flow isse chehra lock karta hai.

```
Photorealistic vertical portrait, 9:16, of a 24-year-old South Asian young woman,
warm wheat-brown skin with natural texture, visible pores and faint dark circles,
oval face, soft full cheeks, small nose, dark brown almond-shaped eyes with subtle
kohl liner, thick natural eyebrows, long black hair in a loose low braid with a few
strands falling on her face, small gold jhumka earrings, tiny gold nose stud on her
left nostril, wearing a plain mustard-yellow cotton kurta with a thin maroon dupatta
loosely draped over one shoulder, no makeup look, simple and real.

She faces the camera directly, calm gentle expression, lips closed, relaxed
shoulders, chest-up medium close-up framing.

Background: plain soft warm cream wall, completely out of focus, no objects, no clutter.
Lighting: soft warm tungsten light from the upper left, gentle fill on the right,
natural warm colour temperature, soft shadows under the cheekbones.

Style: candid UGC smartphone selfie photograph, realistic skin, no beauty filter,
no smoothing, no glamour retouching, slight film grain, natural colour grade.
No text, no captions, no watermark, no logo, no jewellery overload.
```

**Chun-ne ka criteria:**
- ✅ Dono aankhein lens me, chehra seedha, gardan na jhuki ho
- ✅ Skin texture dikh rahi ho (bilkul smooth = plastic lagta hai)
- ✅ Background bilkul saaf aur plain
- ❌ Reject: heavy makeup, smile me daant zyada, side angle, busy background

## INGREDIENT 2 — `noor-three-quarter` (expression range ke liye)

Kahani sunate waqt ke natural expressions.

```
Photorealistic vertical portrait, 9:16, of the same 24-year-old South Asian young
woman — warm wheat-brown skin with natural texture and faint dark circles, oval face,
dark brown almond eyes with subtle kohl liner, thick natural eyebrows, long black hair
in a loose low braid, small gold jhumka earrings, tiny gold nose stud on her left
nostril, plain mustard-yellow cotton kurta with a thin maroon dupatta over one shoulder.

She is turned three-quarters toward the camera, looking slightly off to the side,
warm half-smile, eyes soft and a little thoughtful, one hand raised near her chest
as if telling a story to a friend, light henna visible on her hands.

Background: a blurred cozy corner of a modest South Asian home, warm cream textured
wall, soft warm fairy lights as bokeh, edge of a wooden bookshelf.
Lighting: warm evening tungsten glow from the left, soft rim light on her hair,
shallow depth of field, f/1.8 look.

Style: candid UGC video still, realistic, natural skin texture, no beauty filter,
slight film grain, warm colour grade.
No text, no captions, no watermark, no logo.
```

## INGREDIENT 3 — `noor-full-seated` (wide shots + gesture ke liye)

Poora setting ke saath, jab haath aur body language chahiye.

```
Photorealistic vertical image, 9:16, of the same 24-year-old South Asian young woman
— warm wheat-brown skin, oval face, dark brown almond eyes with subtle kohl liner,
long black hair in a loose low braid, small gold jhumka earrings, tiny gold nose stud,
plain mustard-yellow cotton kurta with a thin maroon dupatta loosely over one shoulder.

She is sitting cross-legged on a low wooden stool in a cozy corner of a modest
South Asian home, both hands wrapped around a small steel cup of chai, light henna
on her hands, leaning slightly forward toward the camera as if about to say
something important, gentle serious expression.

Setting: warm cream textured wall, a wooden shelf with old books behind her,
a small brass oil lamp, soft warm fairy lights blurred in the background,
a thin curtain with faint evening light coming through.
Lighting: warm tungsten practical lamp from the left, soft fill, cozy evening mood.
Composition: medium wide shot, she occupies the lower two-thirds of the vertical frame,
headroom small, shallow depth of field with background out of focus.

Style: candid UGC smartphone video still, realistic, natural skin texture,
no beauty filter, slight film grain, warm colour grade.
No text, no captions, no watermark, no logo.
```

## INGREDIENT 4 (optional) — `corner-background` (ENVIRONMENT lock)

Agar aap chahti ho ke background har video me bilkul same rahe,
ye image alag ingredient ke tor pe banao aur slot 2 me daalo.

```
Photorealistic vertical interior photograph, 9:16, of an empty cozy corner of a
modest South Asian home. No people in the frame.

Warm cream textured plaster wall, a low wooden shelf holding a few old hardcover
books and a small brass oil lamp, soft warm fairy lights strung loosely along the
wall creating gentle bokeh, a thin cotton curtain on the right with faint evening
light filtering through, a small low wooden stool and a steel cup of chai on a
side table in the foreground, slightly cluttered and lived-in.

Lighting: warm tungsten glow from a practical lamp on the left, soft amber
colour temperature, gentle shadows, cozy evening mood.
Composition: eye-level medium wide, the wall fills the upper half, depth of field
shallow with the foreground softly blurred.

Style: realistic UGC smartphone photo, natural grain, warm colour grade,
no cinematic filter, no stylisation.
No text, no captions, no watermark, no logo.
```

## Bonus: emotion variants (jab zaroorat pade)

Same character description rakho, sirf expression line badlo:

| Emotion | Replace with |
|---|---|
| **Dukhi / bheegi aankhein** | `eyes slightly watery and red-rimmed, lower lip trembling, looking down, one hand touching her cheek` |
| **Gussa / insaaf** | `sharp direct gaze into the lens, jaw set, eyebrows slightly lowered, leaning forward, one finger raised` |
| **Khush / chhoti khushi** | `wide genuine smile with eyes crinkled, head tilted slightly, both hands raised near her face in delight` |
| **Hairat / twist** | `eyes wide, mouth slightly open, hand paused mid-air near her chest, frozen in realisation` |
| **Sukoon / shukr** | `eyes softly closed, calm deep breath, both hands resting on the chai cup, faint peaceful smile` |
| **Neechi nazar / pachtawa** | `gaze lowered to her hands, shoulders slightly dropped, quiet expression, holding a steel cup` |

## ⚠️ Common mistakes (ye mat karna)

| Galti | Nateeja | Theek |
|---|---|---|
| Sirf text prompt se video, ingredient ke bina | Har clip me alag chehra | Hamesha `noor-front` slot 1 me |
| Ingredient image me busy background | Flow background bhi copy kar leta hai | Plain cream wall |
| Ingredient image me heavy makeup | Video me bhi makeup aa jata hai | "no makeup look" |
| Alag din pe alag ingredient banana | Skin tone mismatch | Ek hi session me sab |
| 3 se zyada ingredients | Slot limit, style conflict | Max 3: Noor + corner + (prop) |
| Style ingredient (anime/cinematic) mix karna | Face drift | Sab photorealistic rakho |
