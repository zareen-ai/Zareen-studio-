# 02 — Shot Library (reusable Flow prompts)

> Ye 10 shot prompts channel ka toolkit hain. Har episode in me se hi banega —
> sirf ACTION aur DIALOGUE ki line badalni hai. Baaki sab copy-paste.

**Har prompt se pehle Flow me ye attach karo:**
`@noor-front` (slot 1) + `@corner-background` (slot 2) + optional prop (slot 3)

**Settings:** `9:16` · `8 seconds` · Model: **Veo 3.1 Fast** (test) → **Quality** (final)

---

## SHOT A1 — Selfie MCU (HOOK / CTA ke liye)

```
The woman from image 1, vertical 9:16 medium close-up, chest-up framing, handheld
selfie energy with subtle natural camera shake, as if she is holding the phone herself.

She looks directly into the lens and [ACTION — e.g. leans in slightly, raises one
finger, tilts her head].

She is sitting in the cozy corner from image 2 — warm cream textured wall, blurred
warm fairy lights, wooden shelf with old books behind her.

Lighting: warm tungsten glow from the upper left, soft amber colour temperature,
gentle shadow on the right side of her face, shallow depth of field, background
softly out of focus.

Camera: locked handheld feel, no zoom, no pan, tiny organic micro-movements.

Audio: [DIALOGUE — "..." in her warm young South Asian female voice] / OR
no audible dialogue, lips moving naturally as she speaks softly, quiet room tone,
distant street sounds outside, no music.

Style: realistic UGC smartphone video, natural skin texture with visible pores,
no beauty filter, no smoothing, warm colour grade, slight film grain, authentic
and unpolished.
No text, no captions, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no on-screen UI.
One continuous shot, preserve her face, hair, outfit and earrings exactly as the reference.
```

## SHOT D1 — Hand / Prop Insert (beat change, bina chehre ke)

Chehra dikhane ki zaroorat nahi → AI artifacts ka risk bhi kam. Bahut reliable shot.

```
Vertical 9:16 close-up insert shot, no face in frame.

[ACTION — e.g. a young woman's hands with light henna wrap around a small steel cup
of chai, steam rising slowly / her fingers turn the page of an old diary / her hand
places a folded five-hundred rupee note on a wooden counter].

Setting: a low wooden table in a cozy South Asian home, warm cream wall blurred
behind, soft fairy-light bokeh.

Lighting: warm tungsten lamp from the left, amber colour temperature, soft highlight
on the steam / paper / metal surface, shallow depth of field.

Camera: locked macro-ish close-up, one very slow push-in, no shake.

Audio: [SFX — e.g. the soft clink of steel on wood, a quiet page turn, chai being
poured], room tone, no dialogue, no music.

Style: realistic UGC insert footage, natural textures, warm colour grade, slight grain.
No text, no captions, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no distorted hands,
exactly five fingers on each hand.
One continuous shot.
```

## SHOT E1 — B-roll: kahani ka scene (chehre ke bina)

Jab kahani me doosra kirdaar ho (sabziwala, mazdoor, bachcha).
**Noor ki ingredient mat lagao** — naya anonymous character banao, face chhupa ke.

```
Vertical 9:16 medium shot, no face clearly visible — the subject is framed from the
chest down or seen from behind, or their face is turned away / in shadow.

[SUBJECT + ACTION — e.g. an elderly vegetable vendor's weathered hands weigh
tomatoes on an old metal scale at a street stall / a small girl in a school uniform
walks away down a narrow lane holding a broken doll / a rickshaw puller's back as
he pedals away in light rain].

Setting: [a narrow South Asian street lane at dusk, tangled overhead wires, a
painted shutter, a hand-cart with vegetables].

Lighting: warm golden-hour street light, long soft shadows, dust in the air,
slightly hazy.

Camera: [locked tripod / slow tracking shot following from behind], no zoom.

Audio: [street ambience — bicycle bells, distant vendor calls, a passing
motorbike], no dialogue, no music.

Style: realistic documentary UGC footage, natural texture, warm colour grade,
slight handheld grain.
No text, no captions, no signage text, no logos, no watermark.
One continuous shot.
```

**Kyun face chhupana?** Do chehre consistent rakhna AI ke liye mushkil hai.
Bina chehre ka B-roll emotional bhi hota hai aur reliable bhi — aur audience
khud imagine karti hai, jo zyada powerful hai.

## Shot → beat mapping (har episode ke liye)

| Beat | Time | Shot | Kyun |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 0–3s | **A1** | Chehra lens me = ruk jaata hai |
| SETUP | 3–10s | **B1** | Kahani shuru, stable |
| SETUP 2 | 10–18s | **D1 / E1** | Beat change, retention refresh |
| CONFLICT | 18–26s | **B1** (push-in) | Wapas chehra, emotion badhta hai |
| TWIST | 26–34s | **C1** | Tight CU = chot |
| MORAL | 34–42s | **G1** | Sukoon, chai, dheemi awaaz |
| CTA | 42–48s | **A1** | Wapas direct, sawal |

## Prompt debugging

| Problem | Wajah | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Chehra badal gaya | Ingredient attach nahi / slot galat | `noor-front` ko slot 1 me, prompt me "the woman from image 1" |
| Kapde badal gaye | Prompt me outfit describe nahi | Full outfit line har prompt me likho |
| Background alag | Corner ingredient nahi lagaya | Image 2 attach karo |
| Ajeeb text screen pe | Model ne khud caption bana diya | `no text, no captions, no subtitles, no watermark` hamesha |
| Haath 6 ungli | Common AI artifact | Haath wale shot me `exactly five fingers on each hand` + face-out framing |
| Dialogue adhura kat gaya | Line lambi thi | 8s clip me ≤ 12 words |
| Awaz har clip me alag | Native dialogue inconsistent | Option B (silent video + alag VO) use karo |
| Camera zyada hilta | "handheld" zyada strong | `subtle micro-movements` likho, `locked tripod` use karo |
| Video slow/boring | Action nahi likha | Ek clear action verb do: `leans in`, `turns her palms up` |
| Chehra plastic smooth | Beauty filter | `natural skin texture, visible pores, no beauty filter, no smoothing` |
